# Pitch: LatencyLens

> Your speed test says your internet is fast. Your video calls say otherwise.

LatencyLens examines the gap between idle latency and loaded latency. It visualizes how delays change under demand, calculates a transparent heuristic score, and uses Gemma 4 to translate measurements into accessible, testable advice.

**Why AI:** Plain speed tests expose data. Gemma 4 interprets evidence, caveats, and recommended verification steps. It cannot change the numbers or fabricate sensors.

**Why Temporal:** Robust multi-step diagnosis with retries and an auditable workflow ID. A flaky model call doesn't need to destroy the whole session.

**Why Kepler:** The tool that helps developers troubleshoot and recover the infrastructure powering the application. Demonstrate real devops value by diagnosing a Worker failure.

**Why open source:** The scoring logic, probe implementation, API contracts, and orchestration are all public for review. Extensible later to 5G/edge, controlled-load experiments and native measurements.

**Core differentiator:** evidence-to-action, explicit provenance, before-and-after testing in next version.
